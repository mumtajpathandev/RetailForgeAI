# RetailForge AI Promo Video Script

## 30-second vertical cut

**0–3s** — Fast cuts of scattered spreadsheets, trend tabs, return notes, and product cards. On-screen text: “Retail decisions are still fragmented.”

**3–8s** — The RetailForge command center assembles the signals into one clean workspace. Text: “Bring trend, product, margin, and returns together.”

**8–14s** — Trend intelligence card shows “Sculpted utility” with evidence and confidence. Text: “See what is accelerating—and why.”

**14–20s** — Returns intelligence card highlights “Contour Knit Legging” and “Waistband compression.” Text: “Turn return reasons into product action.”

**20–25s** — Line plan and margin scenario animate side by side. Text: “Compare scenarios before committing.”

**25–30s** — Publisher view appears with read-only badge. End card: “RetailForge AI — Evidence-led merchandising decisions.” CTA: “Explore the demo.”

## Voiceover

“RetailForge AI connects the signals behind better retail decisions. See what is accelerating, understand why products come back, compare margin scenarios, and move from AI proposal to human-approved action. RetailForge AI: evidence-led merchandising, from signal to assortment.”
