# RetailForge AI Business Plan

## Problem

Retail teams lose margin when trend signals, product development, return reasons, supplier constraints, and line-plan decisions live in disconnected systems. Existing workflows are slow, difficult to audit, and often treat AI suggestions as opaque answers.

## Product

RetailForge AI is a merchandising operating system that connects retail inputs to evidence-backed decisions. It combines trend intelligence, product and concept workflows, visual line planning, sourcing and margin scenarios, returns intelligence, review queues, integrations, and controlled publisher views.

## Target customers

The initial beachhead is mid-market fashion, activewear, footwear, and lifestyle brands with multi-channel assortments and lean merchandising teams. Buyers include heads of merchandising, product, design, sourcing, planning, and digital commerce.

## Differentiation

RetailForge is designed around traceability: every recommendation should show its evidence, confidence, assumptions, and reviewer. The product also connects forward-looking trend and assortment decisions with backward-looking return signals, which creates a feedback loop rather than another isolated dashboard.

## Revenue model

Use a workspace subscription with tiered limits for brands, seats, data volume, connector count, AI analysis, and publisher links. Offer onboarding and integration services for enterprise customers. Keep a pilot tier for controlled evaluation, but reserve live connectors, advanced governance, and higher data limits for paid plans.

## Go-to-market

Start with design partners in activewear and fashion. Demonstrate a return-rate reduction or margin-improvement workflow using the sample data. Publish evidence-led case studies, short product videos, and practical merchandising playbooks. Sell through founder-led demos, retail consultants, and PLM/ERP implementation partners.

## Key metrics

Track time to first insight, weekly active merchandising users, percentage of recommendations reviewed, import freshness, return-rate reduction, gross-margin improvement, publisher engagement, connector success rate, and conversion from pilot to paid workspace.

## Risks and mitigations

Data quality is addressed with validation and quarantine. AI trust is addressed with evidence, confidence, human approval, and audit logs. Connector fragility is addressed with adapter isolation and replayable imports. Free hosting is limited to testing; production requires durable data, secrets, backups, and operational monitoring.
